#  Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes bank marketing call data to understand customer drop-offs and improve conversions.A marketing funnel dashboard was created using Python and Excel.The analysis showed major drop-offs at the final stage and better performance from mobile calls.It also helped improve overall campaign strategy.

##  Objectives
* Find the funnel stage where interested bank leads drop off.
* Identify whether cellular or landline calls bring high-quality conversions.
* Analyze campaign call frequency to stop customer irritation.
* Track monthly conversion trends to find peak performance seasons.

##  Tools Used
* Python: Cleaned and structured the raw bank lead data.
* Excel: Analyzed performance and built the final dashboard.

##  Key KPIs Mapped
* Total Outreach Volume: 45,211 Calls
* Prospect Engagement Rate: 68.94%
* Account Conversions Count: 5,289 Customers
* Blended Funnel Efficiency: 11.70%
* Peak Conversion Velocity: 51.99%

##  Key Insights
* **83% Final Drop-off:** 31k prospects stay on call over 2 minutes, but only 5k convert. Closing deals is our biggest bottleneck.
* **Mobile Calls Win:** Cellular outreach achieves the highest conversion at 14.92% vs. unknown channels at 4.07%.
* **4+ Calls Fatigue:** Success velocity drops sharply after the 3rd attempt, falling from 14.6% to near 5% by the 10th touchpoint.
* **High Seasonality:** Conversions peak sharply in March (51.99%), Sept (46.46%), Oct (43.77%), & Dec (46.73%), but bottom out in May at just 6.72%.

## Strategic Recommendations
* **Retrain Sales Pitch:** Give phone agents new closing scripts to handle customer doubts and stop the 83% end-of-funnel leakage.
* **Enforce a 3-Call Cap:** Set a strict automated software dialer limit of a maximum of 3 calls per customer to stop wasting staff hours.
* **Shift Campaign Budget:** Stop mass calling in low-performance month like May; double down resources during peak seasons.

## Outcome

* Identified major conversion gap in the final stage, making deal closure the biggest challenge  
* Mobile calls perform best with highest conversion (14.92%)  
* Conversion drops after 3 call attempts due to customer fatigue  
* Found seasonal peaks (March, Sept, Oct, Dec) for better targeting  
* Provided strategies to improve conversions, save time, and boost revenue
